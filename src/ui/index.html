Where the forest meets the tide

Field notes from the Pacific Northwest

At low tide, the coast reveals a landscape that seems to belong to another world. Kelp drapes over dark rocks, and the tide pools hold small reflections of the sky.

Beyond the tree line

The trail winds past stands of Sitka spruce before turning toward the ocean. As the trees thin out, the air takes on the scent of salt, and the sound of the surf grows louder.

On a clear morning, the horizon is almost indistinguishable from the water. Walk slowly enough, and familiar details become reasons to stop.
